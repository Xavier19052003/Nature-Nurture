# Quality content, product UX and image performance release

Validated on 20 October 2026 in the main `Nature Nurture` checkout on `main`.

## Content move and design

The entire About block headed **Developed Around Real Consumer Needs** was removed from About, including its introduction and all four What We Do cards: Product Development, Manufacturing & Co-Manufacturing, Multi-Channel Distribution, and Private Label & Partnerships.

The following large **Manufacturing & Co-Manufacturing** block also moved in full: manufacturing-facility imagery, both explanatory paragraphs, Learn More link, and Quality from the Start subsection. Both blocks now appear on `/certifications`, immediately after Compliance-First Manufacturing and before Manufacturing Excellence. The manufacturing banner retains its complete composition; its copy and Quality from the Start panel sit below it and stack on smaller screens. Learn More still leads to Retailers. The former link to the Quality page now points to the quality-framework anchor within that page.

Duplicate ongoing Batch Testing content was consolidated into the existing Third-Party Testing card. Full Traceability wording was merged into Batch Traceability instead of retaining a second traceability card. Ingredient Sourcing and Product Integrity remain under Our Ongoing Commitment. The original category/market qualifiers, accreditation language and registration numbers were retained.

About retains its story, mission, portfolio, purpose, accessibility, retail and reach narrative. Warm cream values, a sage retail section, a lighter reach section and the existing dark/image bands give the remaining sequence contrast without adding filler or blank space.

New hover effects apply only to the moved four capability cards and the six Manufacturing Excellence cards on Quality: a 4 px lift with restrained border/shadow changes over 300 ms. They require a fine pointer, hover support and no reduced-motion preference. Accreditation/logo tiles, regulatory cards, framework badges, ongoing-commitment panels, About value/portfolio/number cards, nutrition panels, footer and ordinary static information groups did not receive these new hover effects. The existing reveal system remains active on the moved content.

## Carousels

`useAutoplay.ts` owns one lifecycle-cleaned timer at **3000 ms**. Next, Previous, dots, tabs, keyboard navigation and relevant touch interactions reset its full interval. Focus retained on an arrow no longer permanently disables rotation. Offscreen/document-hidden states and reduced-motion preferences suspend rotation; re-entry resumes with a fresh interval. The Zingo hero is automatic only at the mobile breakpoint; desktop retains its gallery arrangement.

Audited components/groups:

- `RangeCarousel`: Sublingual Nutrition, Infant Formula, Cereal & Meal Replacement, Family Protection on Products.
- `CerealRange`: five-flavour tab selector.
- `ZingoProduct`: collection/flavour selector and mobile hero gallery.

Timed browser checks covered automatic advance, Next, Previous, repeated Next clicks, a fresh interval, exactly one subsequent advance, continued cycling and dot selection on each catalogue range. Cereal and Zingo selectors were checked after manual tab/thumbnail/collection selection. Mobile Zingo arrows were retested after correcting an intermediate smooth-scroll event that could overwrite the selected index. Repeated mobile hero arrow clicks also passed the single-advance and continued-cycle checks. Range transitions remain 450 ms; cereal/Zingo entry transitions use 400 ms. Reduced-motion and cleanup behavior were additionally reviewed in source.

These are static asset-set comparisons, not a measured cold-network or LCP speedup. Original files remain available, so the total production directory/archive grows; the active photographic delivery paths use the lighter files.

`scripts/assets/optimize_images.py` uses Pillow quality 86/method 6 and produces widths 480, 800, 1200 where applicable, plus a maximum constrained to the source: up to 1920 px for landscape imagery and 1280 px for portrait/square imagery. `imageAssets.json` records dimensions and variants. `OptimizedImage` supplies native `srcSet`, `sizes`, dimensions and asynchronous decoding. Its default is lazy loading. Existing explicit Zingo/story `srcSet` configurations are preserved. No runtime image-processing service was added.

Page heroes and the first Zingo hero image retain high priority; the small Home category previews, visible brand collage and second visible infant pack are eager without promoting every image to high priority. Existing Home CSS background WebP imagery remains unchanged. Lower editorial images, About/Quality illustrations, product/related cards and most flavour imagery are lazy. Active/next catalogue slides become eager only while their carousel is visible and rotating; cereal/Zingo prepare the next image only while playing.

Browser checks on 17 routes found no photographic originals being delivered as large PNG/JPEG files; logos were excluded from that comparison. Representative lower imagery remained deferred. Optimized B12/product-label and complete manufacturing-banner images were visually inspected for legibility/composition. Responsive candidates support high-density product displays without inventing pixels beyond the original source. Physical-device Retina testing and live production timing were not performed.
