## Stevia

The master catalogue `Natures-Nurture-NEW.pdf` confirms `STEVIA SACHETS` as a product line on pages 1, 2, 4, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, and 16. No distinct flavours or additional sellable variants were confirmed, so it remains a single line-level product rather than an invented variant set.

## Needs manual verification

- Several source pages use promotional composites rather than isolated packaging images.
- Confirm final manufactured pack counts for the mosquito patch variants.
- Confirm whether any cereal or Zingo pack sizes shown in lifestyle scenes are real SKUs; they were not added as variants.
- Confirm final regulatory artwork, net weights, bilingual copy, and commercial SKU identifiers before publication.
